How to Use This Extension

Quick Start
1 Set up your profile — click Edit Profile, fill in your details using the required format (Name, Role, Target Hourly Rate, Executive Summary, Technical Skills), then click Save Profile.
2 Go to an Upwork job page — navigate to any job posting on upwork.com.
3 Click the extension icon in Chrome's toolbar to open the popup, then click Get Details to extract the job information.
4 Click Analyze to get a verdict (Apply / Skip / Apply with Caution), match score, and AI-powered analysis.
5 Toggle Write Proposal + Bid Recommendation to also generate a customized cover letter with bid advice.
Features
  • Job Extraction — automatically grabs title, budget, duration, skills, description, and client info from the Upwork page you are viewing.
  • AI Job Analysis — verdict, match score (1–10), and detailed breakdown of pros, cons, and red flags.
  • Proposal Writer — generates a structured 5-move cover letter with bid recommendation. Toggle it on before analyzing.
  • Profile Matching — compares your skills and rate against the job requirements for a personalized score.
  • Analysis History — past jobs are saved locally so you can revisit previous verdicts anytime.
  • JSON Export — copy raw job data to clipboard for your own records or tools.
Do's & Don'ts
Do
  • Fill in your profile completely before analyzing
  • Analyze jobs before submitting a proposal
  • Use proposal output as a draft base — personalize it
  • Check the History panel to revisit past jobs
Don't
  • Submit the generated proposal without customizing it
  • Analyze a job without setting up your profile first
  • Rely solely on the AI verdict — always do your own research
  • Share your API key or server credentials

Your data stays private. This extension does not store or transmit your data to any server. Job data and your profile are sent directly to the LLM/AI provider for analysis only — the AI processes it and returns the result. No browsing history, credentials, or personal information is collected or retained.
